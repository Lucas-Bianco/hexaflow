# Direction C: photo-led editorial

**Idea.** The project told as a long-read feature in a science magazine: a full-bleed team photo opening, nine numbered chapters where a sticky photograph changes as each journey chapter scrolls past, and a full-screen scrub through the real drop-tower frames. Photography and the team's own journey text carry the page; type does the hierarchy.

**Fonts.** Instrument Serif (display, chapter numbers, pull quote) and Public Sans (text, labels).

**Colours.** Warm near-black `#131211`, panel `#1b1a18`, ink `#ebe6db`, secondary `#b4ad9f`, rules `#34312c`. One accent: desaturated team gold `#b99a55`, used only for small labels, chapter numbers and story progress.

**Scroll moments (only two).**
1. Home: the image beside the journey text crossfades as each of the nine chapters passes, with a gold progress line and a 01 / 09 counter. No parallax.
2. Drop Testing: a sticky full-screen scrub through 18 real frames (Drop 1 or Drop 3 selectable), with four chapter-style captions drawn from dropTests.js text. Everything else is static.

**Why it does not look AI-generated.** Real photographs and real captions with credits lead every section; layouts are asymmetric text-beside-image columns with a numbered chapter list instead of a pill navbar; there are no gradients other than a plain dark scrim for legibility, no glow, no cards, no icons, and one accent colour. Copy is the team's own journey text.
